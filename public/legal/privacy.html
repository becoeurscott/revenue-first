This policy explains how [COMPANY LEGAL NAME], [REGISTERED ADDRESS] ("Vaulten", "we", "us") collects, uses and shares information when you use the Vaulten app and related services (the "Service"). We are the controller of your personal data. Questions: support@vaulten.space.

The short version. We collect what we need to run your account and build your plan: your account details, your onboarding answers and the progress you track. We use trusted providers for hosting, AI, analytics, crash reports and subscriptions. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking. You can delete your account and data at any time.

1. Information we collect

You give us:

  • Account details: name, email address and password (stored only in hashed form).
  • Onboarding answers: how you want to earn, your niche, where you are starting, available time, budget, skills, equipment, general location (such as country or city if you enter it) and your goal.
  • Things you track in the app: missions completed, notes, content or product ideas, numbers you log (such as views, subscribers, sales or income), prospects and deals you add, and weekly check-ins.
  • Messages to the AI Coach and support: the questions you ask and messages you send us.

Collected automatically:

  • Usage data: which screens and features you use and events such as finishing onboarding or a mission, linked to your account ID.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports, error logs and performance data, including device model, operating system and app version.
  • Purchase data: your subscription status, plan, renewal dates and an anonymous App Store transaction ID. We never receive your card or bank details; Apple handles payment.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, or advertising identifiers (IDFA).

Some of the information you track (for example names and details of businesses or creators you add as prospects) may relate to other people. Only add information you are allowed to use, and keep it to business contact details.

3. AI processing

To generate AI Coach answers, your personal program and niche suggestions, we send the relevant inputs (your question, your journey, niche, starting stage, goal and recent progress) to our AI provider, Anthropic, which processes them on our behalf. Under its commercial terms, the provider does not use this data to train its models. Please do not share sensitive information (such as health, financial account or government ID details) with the AI Coach.

4. Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers who process it for us under contract, and only as needed to run the Service:

  • Hosting and backend infrastructure for your account and data;
  • Anthropic: AI responses;
  • Vercel: hosting of our AI endpoints;
  • RevenueCat: subscription management;
  • Apple: App Store payments (under Apple's own privacy policy);
  • PostHog: product analytics;
  • Sentry: crash and error reporting;
  • Email delivery provider: account and verification emails.

We may also disclose information if required by law or a valid legal request, to protect the rights, safety and property of our users or Vaulten, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets (in which case this policy will continue to apply or you will be notified). We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

5. International transfers

Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where data from the EEA, UK or Switzerland is transferred, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

6. How long we keep information

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except where we must keep some of it longer for legal, tax, security or fraud-prevention reasons. Diagnostic and analytics data are kept for up to 12 months. Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access, correction, export and deletion: you can edit your details in the app, export your data from Settings, and delete your account in Settings or by emailing support@vaulten.space.
  • Notifications: turn reminders on or off in Settings or your device settings.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel them in your Apple ID settings. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription.

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, California and other U.S. states), you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and not to be discriminated against for exercising your rights. To make a request, email support@vaulten.space. We may need to verify your identity. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and access tokens stored in your device's secure Keychain. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

Vaulten is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has given us personal data, contact support@vaulten.space and we will delete it.
