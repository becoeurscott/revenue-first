Support

We usually reply within 2 business days.

Email us: support@vaulten.space
In the app you can also go to Profile → Help to send a message or report a problem.

Subscriptions and billing

Vaulten subscriptions are billed by Apple. To cancel or change your plan, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone, or follow Apple's guide. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. To restore a purchase on a new device, tap Restore Purchases on the paywall.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account and confirm with your password. This permanently deletes your account, plan and progress. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel it in your Apple settings as described above. If you can't access the app, email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

Your data

You can download a copy of your data in Profile → Settings. See our Privacy Policy for what we collect and why.

AI Coach

Coach replies are generated by AI and can be wrong. They are educational, not financial, legal or tax advice. To report a reply, touch and hold it in the conversation and choose Report this reply, or email us.
